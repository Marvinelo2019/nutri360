# Energy and macronutrient calculations

Status: complete locally and validated by the user in the browser.

## Outcome

Nutrition professionals can calculate and retain an adult patient's estimated resting and total energy needs, apply a clinician-selected goal adjustment, distribute macronutrients, and use the saved targets to start a meal plan.

## Scope

Included:

- Adult calculations from a selected anthropometric measurement.
- Mifflin-St Jeor (1990), revised Harris-Benedict / Roza-Shizgal (1984), and FAO/WHO/UNU (1985, weight-and-height) equations.
- Clinician-selected activity factor and explicit energy adjustment.
- Macronutrient targets by energy percentage or grams per kilogram.
- Immutable input and output snapshots, formula versions, clinical access control, audit events, patient history, and meal-plan prefill.

Excluded:

- Paediatric, pregnancy, lactation, critical-care, and disease-specific equations.
- Automatic prescriptions, recommended deficits or surpluses, indirect calorimetry, food equivalents, and nutrient databases.
- Editing or deleting completed calculations; a correction creates a new calculation.

## Context

- Product and safety requirements: `docs/PRODUCT.md` and `docs/SECURITY.md`.
- Existing adult anthropometry and draft meal-plan targets.
- Primary formula references: Mifflin et al., AJCN 1990; Roza and Shizgal, AJCN 1984; FAO/WHO/UNU Technical Report Series 724, Annex 1.
- Predictive values are estimates and must be labelled as such in the interface.

## Data changes

- Add formula and macro-distribution enums plus an immutable `energy_calculations` table.
- Store patient, source measurement, all calculation inputs, outputs, formula version, author, and timestamp.
- Add clinician-only RLS and a security-definer creation RPC that derives organization ownership and recalculates every output in PostgreSQL.
- Add read audit and cross-organization/role pgTAP coverage.
- Rollback locally through `supabase db reset`; shared environments receive a later forward migration.

## Security and privacy

- Only owners and nutritionists may read or create calculations.
- The RPC derives organization, patient, measurement, age, sex, weight, and height from protected rows.
- Client input is limited to formula, activity factor, goal adjustment, and macro method/parameters; PostgreSQL computes and stores outputs.
- Audit metadata contains identifiers and formula labels, never patient measurements or calculated values.
- No calculation inputs or results are written to application logs.

## Verification

- Known formula fixtures match independently calculated expected results.
- Percentage inputs must total 100; g/kg inputs remain within explicit bounds.
- Minors, patients without reference sex, and mismatched measurements are rejected.
- Assistants and cross-organization users see no rows and cannot invoke creation.
- A saved calculation can prefill the matching patient's draft meal-plan targets.
- Lint, typecheck, unit tests, database tests, formatting, and production build pass.
